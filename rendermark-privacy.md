# Rendermark: Markdown, Mermaid & Code for Confluence, Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

**Who we are.** Rendermark ("the App") is provided by Nathan Shokalook ("we", "us"). Contact: rendermark.support@gmail.com.

## What the App does
The App is a Confluence Cloud macro that runs on Atlassian Forge. It renders Markdown, Mermaid diagrams and code that page authors paste into it.

## Data the App processes
| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| The text you paste into the macro | To render it on the page and in exports | Stored by Confluence as part of the page, like any other page content |

## What the App does **not** do
- We run no servers. The App runs entirely on Atlassian Forge inside your Confluence site.
- The App requests no Atlassian API permissions and makes no network calls outside Atlassian.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- We never see, collect, sell or share your content or personal data.

## Third parties
**Atlassian** hosts and runs the App and stores page content. See https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Retention
Macro content lives in your Confluence pages and follows your site's own retention and deletion. Uninstalling the App does not delete page content.

For questions, contact rendermark.support@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
