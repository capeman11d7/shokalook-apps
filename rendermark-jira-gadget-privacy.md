# Rendermark: Rich Text & Markdown Gadget for Jira, Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

**Who we are.** Rendermark: Rich Text & Markdown Gadget for Jira ("the App") is provided by Nathan Shokalook ("we", "us"). Contact: rendermark.support@gmail.com.

## What the App does
The App is a Jira Cloud dashboard gadget that runs on Atlassian Forge. It displays formatted text (Markdown, tables, callout panels, code and diagrams) that dashboard editors write into it.

## Data the App processes
| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| The text and title you enter in the gadget | To show it on the dashboard | Stored by Jira as the gadget's configuration on your dashboard, like any other gadget setting |

## Permissions
The App requests one Jira permission, **write:dashboard:jira**. It uses it only to set the gadget's title when a dashboard editor saves the gadget, acting as that editor. Jira checks that person's own dashboard permissions.

## What the App does **not** do
- We run no servers. The App runs entirely on Atlassian Forge inside your Jira site, and renders content in your browser.
- The App makes no network calls outside Atlassian.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- We never see, collect, sell or share your content or personal data.

## Third parties
**Atlassian** hosts and runs the App and stores dashboard data. See https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Retention
Gadget content lives in your Jira dashboards and follows your site's own retention and deletion. Removing the gadget removes its content from the dashboard. Uninstalling the App does not delete dashboard data.
