## Why presence matters

Two agents answering the same service request, two developers editing the same description, or a manager watching a ticket during an incident: knowing who else is looking prevents duplicate work and overwritten edits.

## What Onlook does

- **Live avatars** of everyone viewing the issue, in the details area on the right (not at the bottom of the issue), with names.
- **Fast updates:** joins appear in a second or two, and people who leave drop off within a minute. A hidden or minimized tab counts as away.
- **A Jira flag** tells you when someone opens the issue you're on.
- **Recently viewed:** the last 10 viewers in the past 30 days, re-checked against issue permissions every time.
- **Privacy controls:** admins can turn presence off per project, or show names only to Jira and project admins while everyone else sees a count.
- **Light and dark themes.**
- **Private by design:** stores account IDs only, never names or emails, and view history expires after 30 days. Runs on Atlassian Forge with no outside servers.

## Limits to know

- No viewer-count badge on the issue glance yet.
- It shows who is viewing, not who is typing.

## How to set it up

1. Install Onlook from the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/3685747570).
2. Open any issue and expand **Who's looking** in the details area on the right. No setup needed.
3. Optional: a Jira admin opens Onlook's settings to switch projects off or restrict names to admins.

## FAQ

**Can I see who is viewing a Jira issue?**
Not in Jira itself. A presence app like Onlook or Who's Looking adds it.

**Is Who's Looking for Jira still maintained?**
It's an Atlassian Labs app, which Atlassian labels unsupported. Its last release was February 2022.

**Is issue presence a privacy concern?**
It can be, so Onlook lets admins disable it per project or show names only to admins, and it never stores names or emails.

**Does it slow Jira down?**
Onlook uses Atlassian's Forge Realtime service for presence, so there's no polling server.
