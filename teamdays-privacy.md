# Teamdays: Team Calendar for Confluence, Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

**Who we are.** Teamdays ("the App") is provided by Nathan Shokalook, trading as Shokalook Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: rendermark.support@gmail.com.

## What the App does
The App is a Confluence Cloud macro that runs on Atlassian Forge. It shows a shared calendar on a Confluence page and lets people with edit permission on that page add, change and delete events.

## Data the App processes
| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Event details: title, category, start and end, time zone, repeat rule and skipped dates, notes | To show the calendar | Atlassian Forge storage for your site |
| The Atlassian account ID and display name of the person who added an event | To show who added it, and who is away for "Out of office" events | Atlassian Forge storage for your site |
| The ID of the page the calendar belongs to, and the calendar's name and default view | To find the calendar and check permissions | Forge storage, and the page itself (macro settings) |
| Your permissions on that page | To decide whether you can view or edit the calendar | Read from Confluence when needed, not stored |

## What the App does **not** do
- We run no servers. The App runs entirely on Atlassian Forge inside your Atlassian site, and its data stays in Atlassian's Forge storage.
- The App makes no network calls outside Atlassian and connects to no external calendar.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising. The app remembers which categories you hid in your own browser only.
- We never see, collect, sell or share your calendar data or personal data. The .ics export is generated in your browser and saved to your device.

## Atlassian permissions the App requests
- `storage:app`: store events in Forge storage.
- `read:page:confluence`: check whether you can view or edit the page that holds the calendar.
- `read:confluence-user`: read your display name when you add an event.

## Third parties
**Atlassian** hosts and runs the App and stores its data. See https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Retention
Deleted events are removed right away. Calendar data otherwise stays until you delete it. If the App is uninstalled, Atlassian keeps the App's Forge storage for 28 days (so a reinstall can recover it) and then deletes it.
