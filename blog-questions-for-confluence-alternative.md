_By Shokalook Apps, the maker of Answerloop. Updated October 18, 2026. Marketplace figures were checked on October 16, 2026._

**Short answer:** Atlassian's own "Questions for Confluence" is the best-known Q&A app for Confluence, but it is the most expensive option and holds a 3.0★ rating, with reviews that complain about poor search and missing permissions. **Answerloop: Q&A for Confluence** offers questions, answers, votes, accepted answers, tags and search across answer text, respects space permissions, and can import from Questions for Confluence Server/Data Center. It is free for up to 10 users and costs about a seventh of Atlassian's app above that.

## When to choose each

- **Questions for Confluence:** if you want an Atlassian-built app and can pay the premium.
- **Smart Questions and Answers or Community Questions:** well-reviewed smaller apps worth a trial.
- **Answerloop:** if you're migrating from Questions for Confluence Server/Data Center, need questions to follow space permissions, or want the lowest price for a growing team.

## What Answerloop does

- **Ask, answer, vote and accept.** Answers support Markdown with code blocks. The accepted answer is pinned first.
- **Search that covers answers,** not only question titles, plus filters by space, tag and "unanswered".
- **Space permissions:** each question belongs to a space, and people only see and post in spaces they can view.
- **A "Q&A: Questions list" macro** to show the latest questions, or one tag, on any page (for example on an onboarding page).
- **Import from Questions for Confluence Server/Data Center,** keeping topics as tags, accepted answers, dates and author names. Running the import twice skips duplicates.
- **Moderation:** authors edit their own posts; Confluence admins can edit, delete, close and import.
- **Private:** data stays in Atlassian's Forge storage for your site. No AI, no outside servers.

## Limits to know

- No email notifications for new answers yet (on the roadmap).
- Importing from Questions for Confluence **Cloud** isn't possible, because that app has no export or API. Import works from Server/Data Center.
- It's a new app with no reviews yet.

## How to set it up

1. Install Answerloop from the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1708163143).
2. Open **Apps → Questions** in Confluence and ask the first question. No setup needed.
3. Optional: an admin clicks **Import** to bring over questions from a Server/Data Center instance.

## FAQ

**What is the best alternative to Questions for Confluence?**
It depends on budget and migration needs. Answerloop is the lowest-priced option with a Server/Data Center import; Smart Questions and Community Questions have strong early reviews.

**Can I migrate from Questions for Confluence?**
From Server/Data Center, yes: export the questions as JSON from its REST endpoint and import them into Answerloop.

**Does Answerloop respect Confluence permissions?**
Yes. A question in a restricted space stays hidden from people who can't view that space.

**Is there a free Q&A app for Confluence?**
Answerloop is free for sites with up to 10 users.
