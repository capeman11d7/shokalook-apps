# A team calendar in Confluence without Premium: options compared (2026)

_By Shokalook Apps, the maker of Teamdays. Updated October 18, 2026. Marketplace figures were checked on October 16, 2026._

**Short answer:** Atlassian's Team Calendars is a Confluence **Premium** feature, so Standard-plan sites have no built-in shared calendar. Most Marketplace alternatives sync Google or Outlook and need a calendar account for every user. **Teamdays: Team Calendar for Confluence** puts a shared calendar on any page, on every plan, with no external account: meetings, deadlines, releases and out-of-office, with repeating events, drag and drop, dark mode and .ics export. It's free for up to 10 users.

## When to choose each

- **Confluence Premium:** if you're upgrading anyway, Team Calendars is included.
- **Google Calendar, Outlook Calendars or Meetical:** best if your team's calendar already lives in Google or Microsoft 365 and you want it mirrored into Confluence. Meetical is highly rated for meeting notes.
- **Calendar for Confluence (Rozdoum):** free, though reviews mention trouble launching the app.
- **Teamdays:** if you're on Confluence Standard and want a calendar that lives in Confluence itself, with no account to connect.

## What Teamdays does

- **A calendar macro** for any page or blog post. Click a day (or drag across days) to add an event.
- **Color-coded categories:** Meeting, Deadline, Release, Event, Out of office and Other. Click the legend to filter.
- **Out of office** entries show who is away.
- **Repeating events:** weekly, monthly or yearly, every N periods, with an end date and "skip this date".
- **Drag and drop,** month, week and agenda views.
- **Time zones done right:** each viewer sees their own time zone, and a 9:00 meeting stays at 9:00 when the clocks change.
- **.ics export** for Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar.
- **Confluence permissions:** page viewers see the calendar, page editors change it.
- **Private:** events stay in Atlassian's Forge storage for your site. No outside servers, no analytics.

## Limits to know

- No two-way sync with Google or Outlook (export only).
- No attendees or email reminders yet.

## How to set it up

1. Install Teamdays from the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1289891061).
2. Edit a page, type `/Teamdays`, and insert the calendar.
3. Publish the page and click a day to add your first event.

## FAQ

**Is Team Calendars available on Confluence Standard?**
Atlassian lists Team Calendars as a Premium feature. On Standard, use a Marketplace app.

**How do I track vacations and PTO in Confluence?**
Use a calendar app with an out-of-office category. In Teamdays, add an "Out of office" event and it shows who is away.

**Can I add a calendar to a Confluence page without Google Calendar?**
Yes. Teamdays stores events in Confluence and needs no Google or Microsoft account.

**Can I export the calendar to Outlook?**
Yes, as an .ics file.
