# Answerloop: Q&A for Confluence, Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

**Who we are.** Answerloop ("the App") is provided by Nathan Shokalook ("we", "us"). Contact: rendermark.support@gmail.com.

## What the App does
The App adds community questions and answers to Confluence Cloud. It runs on Atlassian Forge, inside your Confluence site.

## Data the App processes
| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Questions, answers, tags, and the space each question belongs to | To show, search and moderate your Q&A | Forge SQL, Atlassian's hosted database for your site's installation of the App |
| The Atlassian account ID and display name of each author | To show who asked or answered, and to let people edit their own posts | Forge SQL, stored with each post |
| Votes (account ID and up or down) | To allow one vote per person | Forge SQL |
| Author names, dates and content from files an admin imports from Questions for Confluence | To keep the history of imported questions | Forge SQL |
| A list of the account IDs above, with when each name was last read | To report stored personal data to Atlassian every week, as Atlassian requires | Forge SQL |
| The spaces you can view, and whether you are a Confluence admin | To show you only questions from spaces you can view | Fetched from Confluence as you on each visit and held in memory for up to 60 seconds. Never stored |

## What the App does **not** do
- We run no servers. All processing and storage happens on Atlassian's Forge platform.
- The App makes no network calls outside Atlassian, and uses no AI services.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- We do not access, export, sell or share your content or personal data.

## Third parties
**Atlassian** hosts and runs the App and its database. See https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Retention and deletion
- Authors can delete their own questions and answers, and Confluence admins can delete any of them. Deleting a question also deletes its answers and votes.
- Every week the App reports the account IDs it stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting API. When an Atlassian account is closed, the App replaces that person's name on their posts with "Former user", removes the link to their account, and deletes their votes. When someone changes their profile, the App updates the name stored with their posts.
- If the App is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes its Forge storage under Atlassian's data retention rules for Forge apps.
- To have your name removed from posts, ask your site's Confluence admin, or contact us.

## Your rights
For access, correction or deletion requests, contact your Confluence site admin, who controls the content, or email rendermark.support@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
