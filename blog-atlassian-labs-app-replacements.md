## What "unsupported" means in practice

- Bugs may never be fixed, and there's no one to email when something breaks.
- The app can be retired. Teams then need to migrate in a hurry.
- Security reviews are harder, because there's no vendor to answer a questionnaire.

## What the replacements have in common

- **Free for up to 10 users,** then priced per user below most paid competitors.
- **Built on Atlassian Forge** and running entirely on Atlassian: no outside servers, no tracking, no AI processing of your data.
- **Light and dark mode** with Atlassian design tokens.
- **A support address** that answers: rendermark.support@gmail.com.

## FAQ

**Are Atlassian Labs apps safe to use?**
They're built by Atlassian, but labeled unsupported. Many teams use them; the risk is no fixes and possible retirement.

**Will my data move over?**
Rendermark renders the same Markdown and Mermaid text, so you can paste existing content in. Remindly, Onlook and the Jira gadget start fresh. Answerloop can import from Questions for Confluence Server/Data Center.
