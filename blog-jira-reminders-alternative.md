# Jira issue reminders: alternatives to My Reminders for Jira compared (2026)

_By Shokalook Apps, the maker of Remindly. Updated October 18, 2026. Marketplace figures were checked on October 16, 2026._

**Short answer:** Jira Cloud has no built-in "remind me about this issue" feature. The most installed app, Atlassian Labs' free "My Reminders", is unsupported and only lets you remind yourself. **Remindly: Issue Reminders for Jira** reminds yourself, the assignee, the reporter, teammates or a whole group, at an exact time in any time zone, with repeats, snooze and 90 days of history. It's free for up to 10 users.

## When to choose each

- **My Reminders:** free and simple, if reminding only yourself is enough.
- **Teamlead or Starware:** established, well-reviewed paid apps.
- **Boomerang or Simple Reminder:** the cheapest paid options.
- **Remindly:** if you need to remind other people or groups, pick exact times instead of hour-long windows, or see a history of what was sent.

## What Remindly does

- **Remind anyone:** yourself, the assignee, the reporter, any teammate or a group. Admins decide who may remind others.
- **Exact times:** any 15-minute slot in any time zone, in 12- or 24-hour format. Quick picks for "in 1 hour", "tomorrow 9:00" and "next Monday 9:00".
- **Repeats:** daily, weekly or monthly. A 9:00 reminder stays at 9:00 across daylight-saving changes, and monthly reminders on the 31st land on the last day of shorter months.
- **Snooze** 15 minutes, 1 hour or until tomorrow.
- **A Reminders page** with Upcoming, Set for others and History (90 days) tabs.
- **Per-project switch** for admins, and light and dark mode.
- **Private:** emails go out through Jira's own notifications, only to people who can see the issue. No outside servers or tracking.

## Limits to know

- Notifications are Jira emails plus a banner on the issue; there's no Slack or Teams delivery.
- Reminders relative to the due date ("2 days before due") aren't available yet.

## How to set it up

1. Install Remindly from the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/484018714).
2. Open any issue, find the **Reminders** panel, pick a time and recipients, and save.

## FAQ

**How do I set a reminder on a Jira issue?**
Install a reminder app. With Remindly, open the issue's Reminders panel and pick a time.

**Can I remind someone else about a Jira issue?**
Yes, with Remindly or the paid Teamlead and Starware apps. Atlassian's free My Reminders only reminds yourself.

**Can Jira reminders repeat?**
Yes. Remindly repeats daily, weekly or monthly.

**Do reminders work in Jira Service Management?**
Yes. The panel appears on Jira issues, including service requests.
