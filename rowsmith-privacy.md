# Rowsmith: Table Field for Jira, Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

**Who we are.** Rowsmith ("the App") is provided by Nathan Shokalook ("we", "us"). Contact: rendermark.support@gmail.com.

## What the App does
The App adds a Table custom field type to Jira Cloud. It runs on Atlassian Forge, inside your Jira site.

## Data the App processes
| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| The rows you enter in a Table field | To show and edit the table | Jira, as the custom field's value on the issue (the same place Jira keeps every other field) |
| For User columns: the picked person's Atlassian account ID and display name | To show who a row refers to | Jira, inside the field value |
| The column settings an admin chooses | To know which columns a table has | Jira, as the field context's configuration |
| People search results while you pick a user | To suggest names as you type | Fetched from Jira as you, shown in the editor, never stored |

## What the App does **not** do
- We run no servers, and the App has no database of its own. Everything stays in Jira on Atlassian's Forge platform.
- The App makes no network calls outside Atlassian, and uses no AI services.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
- We do not access, export, sell or share your content or personal data.

## Third parties
**Atlassian** hosts Jira and runs the App. See https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Retention and deletion
- Table values live on Jira issues. Editing a row, clearing the field or deleting the issue removes the data, as with any Jira field.
- If you uninstall the App, Jira keeps the field data for 30 days so a reinstall can restore it, then deletes it (Forge platform behaviour).
- Display names in User columns are a snapshot taken when the person was picked. Edit the row to refresh one.

## Your rights
Your Jira site admin controls the data. For questions or requests, email rendermark.support@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.
